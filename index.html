About Best Headliners Plus

Serving the Coastal Empire since 1996.

Andy installed his first headliner in 1996, at just 17 years old, working under the name "Andy Auto Headliners." What started as one guy with the patience to do the job right turned into three decades of steady, hands-on work.

In 2000, Andy trained at ASI (American Sunroof) in Detroit, Michigan, learning to install electric sunroofs and moonroofs from the ground up. The shop still runs on the same rule he started with: honesty and quality first, and treat others the way we want to be treated.
